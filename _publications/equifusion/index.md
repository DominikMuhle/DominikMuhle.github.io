description: "We introduce EquiFusion, the first model for SHMP that generalizes to unseen skeleton parameterization, i.e. kinematics. We unlock training on multiple datasets and inference on motion parametrized with different kinematics. EquiFusion is the first SHMP model to handle zero-shot novel kinematics and occluded limbs without explicit training, while achieving SOTA results on established benchmarks."
